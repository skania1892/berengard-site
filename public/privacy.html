2. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact form. When you submit the form on our home page we collect your name, email address, company name (optional), and the message you write.
  • Arwen chat. When you use the chat widget, we process the text of the messages you send. Please do not type sensitive personal information, confidential business information, passwords, or health or financial data into the chat.
  • Email and other correspondence. If you email us, book a consult, or reply to one of our messages, we keep that correspondence and any information in it.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and request logs. Our host records standard technical data when you load a page: IP address, browser type and version, device and operating system, referring page, the pages you viewed, and timestamps.
  • Rate-limiting data. Our chat function reads your IP address to stop one visitor from flooding the service. It is held only in the server’s temporary memory for a rolling 30-second window and is never written to a database.
  • No third-party trackers. We load no advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts, so no third party collects data about you through this site. See Cookies & tracking.

Information from other sources

For business development we collect publicly available business contact information — company name, website, business phone number, publicly listed business email address, and business address — from sources such as company websites and business listing services including the Google Places API. See Business outreach email.

3. AI processing & Arwen

Arwen, the chat assistant on our site, is powered by a large language model provided by Anthropic, PBC. Here is exactly what happens when you send a message:

  • Your message text is sent from your browser to our server function, and from there to Anthropic’s API together with a fixed set of instructions and our published FAQ content.
  • Anthropic generates a reply and returns it to us, and we display it to you.
  • We do not save the conversation. The transcript exists only in your browser’s memory for the length of your visit and is gone when you close or reload the page. We keep no copy, and there is no database of chat conversations.
  • Anthropic processes the message as our service provider and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. Anthropic may retain it briefly for abuse monitoring. See Anthropic’s Privacy Policy.
  • If something goes wrong, our error logs may record that an error occurred and its type. They are not designed to record your message content.

AI answers can be wrong. Arwen produces automated responses and may be inaccurate or incomplete. It is not professional, legal, financial, or technical advice, and nothing it says creates a binding quote or contract. Please confirm anything important with a human at hello@berengard.tech. We do not use Arwen or any other automated system to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

6. Business outreach email

We contact businesses that we think would benefit from our services. If you received an email from us and are wondering how we got your address, this section is for you.

  • Where we got it. From publicly available business sources — your company website, a public business listing, or a referral. We do not buy contact lists, and we do not target individuals in their personal capacity.
  • What we store. Business contact details, notes about your publicly visible online presence, and a record of our correspondence with you.
  • How to stop it. Reply with “unsubscribe,” “remove me,” or “stop emailing me,” or email hello@berengard.tech. We honor opt-out requests promptly — generally within one business day, and always well inside the ten business days required by the CAN-SPAM Act. Once you opt out we keep the minimum record needed to make sure we never contact you again.
  • Deletion. If you would rather we erase your details entirely instead of suppressing them, say so and we will, though we will then have no record preventing a future contact.

Every marketing email we send identifies us, includes our postal address, and tells you how to opt out.

7. Cookies & tracking

This site sets no cookies at all. We use no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no retargeting tags, no session or preference cookies, and no cross-site tracking of any kind. We load no third-party scripts. There is nothing here to consent to, which is why you will not see a cookie banner.

The Arwen chat assistant keeps your conversation in your browser’s memory only for the length of your visit and writes nothing to cookies or local storage. Close or reload the page and it is gone.

Because we do no tracking whatsoever, there is nothing for a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off, but we honor such signals as opt-out requests where the law requires it.

9. Your privacy rights

Several U.S. states — including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia, Utah, and others — give residents specific privacy rights. Rather than sorting people by state, we extend the following rights to everyone who contacts us, regardless of where you live:

  • Know and access — ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct — ask us to fix information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete — ask us to erase your personal information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Opt out — tell us to stop sending you marketing email.
  • Portability — receive your information in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Non-retaliation — we will never treat you worse for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise a right, email hello@berengard.tech and tell us what you want. We respond within 45 days and will let you know if we need more time. We may need to verify your identity first, usually by confirming you control the email address in question, and we will not ask for more information than is necessary to do so. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written proof of authorization.

If you believe we have mishandled your request, you may appeal by replying to our response with the word “appeal.” We will review it and give you a written answer. Residents of some states may also complain to their state attorney general.

10. No sale of personal information

We do not and will not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. 11. Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect personal information: encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the site, multi-factor authentication on our business accounts, access limited to people who need it, reputable service providers with their own security programs, and a deliberate practice of not collecting or storing data we do not need. Storing no chat transcripts is itself a security measure — data that does not exist cannot be breached.

That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

12. Children’s privacy

Our site and services are intended for businesses and for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email hello@berengard.tech and we will delete it.

13. Client data we handle

When we deliver services, a client may give us access to data that includes personal information about their customers or staff — for example while building a chatbot, an automation, or an analytics dashboard. In those cases we act as a service provider or processor for that client, not as the controller. We use such data only to perform the agreed work and on that client’s instructions, we do not use it for our own purposes or to train AI models, and our written services agreement with the client governs it. If you are an individual whose data one of our clients holds, please contact that business directly — they control it, and we will support them in responding to you.
